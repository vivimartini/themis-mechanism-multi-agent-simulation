## Validation relationship

- `engine.themis` is the readable reference mechanism.
- `rq2.endogenous_coverage` is the fine vectorised strategic solver.
- `rq2.coarse_solver` is the reduced-grid solver used for expensive empirical
  game and sampled-world experiments.
- `rq2.robustness` compares the reference and fine solvers under strategic
  reports; `rq2.validate` runs a smaller release smoke check.
- `rq2.validate_alpharank` validates the finite-game ranking implementation on
  known fixtures.

These are separate implementations with explicit agreement checks, rather than
one literally unchanged solver.

## Interpretation qualification

The submitted dissertation states a `[-60, 60]^2` report-perturbation domain.
The shared CMA-ES oracle in `rq2/oracle.py` is unbounded and generated the
reported results over `R^2`; China's reported coverage perturbation of about
`-70.1` is outside the stated box. This release preserves the submitted
numerical evidence and records the discrepancy instead of retroactively changing
the game.
